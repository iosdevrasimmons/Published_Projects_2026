Shipping & Returns

Information about individually sold McFarlands apparel and digital-book purchases.

Last updated November 24, 2026

Physical apparel

Hoodies, sweaters, T-shirts, trucker hats, and baby bodysuits are individually priced physical goods. Shipping destinations, available methods, amounts, and estimated delivery information must be displayed and confirmed at checkout. We do not promise a fixed delivery date on this website because fulfillment timing depends on the selected item and destination.

Order issues and refunds

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or missing, contact ios.dev.rasimmons@icloud.com with your order reference and a brief description so the issue can be investigated. Refunds, replacements, or other remedies will be determined under applicable consumer law and the conditions communicated at purchase. For discretionary size or preference returns, contact support before shipping anything back; an approved return destination and instructions are required.

Digital edition

No shipping applies to Jewelz the Warrior Princess purchased inside the iPhone app. Apple processes digital-book payments, and requests for In-App Purchase refunds should be submitted through Apple's report-a-problem service. Restoring an eligible purchase is available in the app.

Questions before purchasing

For information about an apparel item's measurements, production timing, or delivery availability, contact support before placing an order. The checkout amount and shipping details shown at the time of payment control.
