Privacy Policy

How the McFarlands website and iPhone shopping app handle customer information.

Last updated November 24, 2026

Who we are

McFarlands is an apparel and digital publishing brand based in Michigan, United States. This policy covers the McFarlands website and McFarlands iPhone app. For privacy questions, contact ios.dev.rasimmons@icloud.com.

Information we handle

The website presents brand, product, and support information. We do not operate an account registration form, advertising tracker, or newsletter signup on this site. Our website host, GitHub Pages, may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser information, to deliver the pages.

In the iPhone app, a shopping bag may hold selected item IDs, quantities, and sizes on your device. For clothing purchases, a checkout request sends those selections to McFarlands' payment server. Payment checkout may collect your name, email, payment information, billing and shipping address, order details, and tax-related location information. When a purchase is completed, order records may be stored to confirm and fulfill the order and handle customer support.

For the digital book, Apple processes In-App Purchase transactions. The app receives the purchase entitlement and limited transaction status needed to unlock or restore your book. McFarlands does not receive your full Apple payment card number.

Payment providers and other services

Physical merchandise purchases use Stripe-hosted Checkout. Stripe handles payment details according to its own privacy practices. Digital book purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Product artwork may be delivered using Stripe-hosted image links. Our static public website is hosted through GitHub Pages.

Provider information: Stripe Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy, and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Why information is used

Information is used to present products, process customer purchases, calculate tax and shipping, confirm and fulfill orders, provide purchased digital content, resolve disputes or payment issues, respond to support requests, and meet applicable financial or legal recordkeeping requirements. We do not sell customer information to advertisers.

Retention and access

Shopping bag information is generally held within the application session. Transaction and order records are retained only as reasonably necessary for order operations, refunds, chargebacks, tax records, security, and legal obligations. Retention periods vary with the record and applicable requirements. Third-party payment providers maintain their own records.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information held by McFarlands, contact ios.dev.rasimmons@icloud.com. Include enough information to locate your request, but do not send card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials. Some information must be retained to comply with law or resolve transactions.

Children and rights

The shopping app is intended for adults. Although some merchandise is designed for children, McFarlands does not knowingly solicit personal information from children through this website. You may have privacy rights under applicable state law, including rights to inquire about data handling or request deletion, subject to exceptions.

Updates

We may revise this policy when our services or data practices change. The revision date appears at the top of this page. Contact us before placing an order if you need clarification about a specific privacy practice.
