## Important limits

- All product names, prices, stock, orders, customers, businesses, charts, and metrics are **illustrative**.
- No real Shopify connection, payment checkout, shipping calculation, database, login, booking system, or audio streaming exists yet.
- Storefront demo bag is browser-only and resets on refresh; it never submits an order.
- Dashboard filters, status changes, and CSV export work in-browser only; exported data is sample data.
- Production launch needs real content, product assets with rights, brand approvals, legal/compliance pages, privacy terms, and authorized integrations.
- Do not commit production API keys, payment secrets, private client/customer details, or unlicensed artwork.

## Customization

Edit text and products in each site's `index.html`, colors in `styles.css`, and interactions in `script.js`. For Shopify, replace the demonstration cart with a supported hosted Shopify checkout integration **after** configuring a real store.
