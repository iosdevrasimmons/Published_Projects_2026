Support

Help with the McFarlands app, clothing purchases, and the Jewelz the Warrior Princess digital edition.

Last updated November 24, 2026

Contact

Email ios.dev.rasimmons@icloud.com for app support, product questions, order concerns, and privacy requests. Please include your order reference if you have one. Never email a full card number or password.

Clothing orders

Use the order confirmation issued through the apparel checkout service as your reference when asking about an order. Clothing purchases are separate-item purchases. For shipping and refunds, see Shipping & Returns.

Digital book

Jewelz the Warrior Princess is a one-time digital-book purchase intended for the McFarlands iPhone app. If you previously purchased it through Apple, use the app's “Restore Previous Purchase” option. Apple handles billing and refund requests for App Store In-App Purchases. For help with an Apple purchase, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Accessibility

If you have difficulty using the app, reading the digital book, or accessing this website, email the support address with your device type and the screen or activity affected. We welcome clear reports and accessibility requests.
