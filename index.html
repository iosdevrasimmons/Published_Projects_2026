The woman behind the brand

Meet Jewel
McFarland.

Jewel McFarland is a mother, fashion designer, and advocate for women and young girls. A survivor of sex trafficking and substance use involving fentanyl and other drugs, she brings her lived experiences into a story of rebuilding, creativity, and purpose.

McFarlands reflects her belief that confidence can grow through every chapter of life. Her fashion collections and the story behind Jewelz the Warrior Princess celebrate individuality, connection, and resilience.

Biography shared for the McFarlands brand. Sensitive biographical details should be approved by Jewel before publication.

Shopping with McFarlands

Are matching pieces sold as a set?

No. Each hoodie, sweater, T-shirt, hat, and baby bodysuit is purchased separately.

Where do I buy the digital book?

The digital edition will be available through the McFarlands iPhone app using Apple's In-App Purchase system, subject to App Store availability.

How do apparel payments work?

The app uses Stripe-hosted checkout for physical merchandise. The final checkout displays applicable taxes and shipping charges.
