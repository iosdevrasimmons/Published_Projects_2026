Using our services

McFarlands provides product descriptions, apparel browsing, brand information, and a digital-book feature. Use the services lawfully and provide accurate information when placing an order.

Individual products

Every apparel listing is for the single named product. Matching sets are styling suggestions only; other garments and accessories are not included unless explicitly stated otherwise. Confirm selected size, color, quantity, and availability before checkout.

Apparel payments

Apparel payments are handled through Stripe Checkout. The applicable checkout screen displays item totals, taxes, and shipping charges before a customer authorizes payment. An order may be declined or canceled if it cannot reasonably be fulfilled; in that case, the affected payment is refunded according to the payment method and applicable law.
